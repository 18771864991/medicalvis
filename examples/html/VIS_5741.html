NLQ:

  1. Create a pie chart showing the count of unique subjects of each gender who have been diagnosed with Candidal esophagitis and have an abnormal lab result.

  2. How many unique subjects of each gender, diagnosed with Candidal esophagitis, have an abnormal lab result, represented in a pie chart?

DVQ:

Visualize PIE SELECT DEMOGRAPHIC.GENDER, COUNT(DISTINCT DEMOGRAPHIC.SUBJECT_ID) FROM DEMOGRAPHIC INNER JOIN DIAGNOSES on DEMOGRAPHIC.HADM_ID = DIAGNOSES.HADM_ID INNER JOIN LAB on DEMOGRAPHIC.HADM_ID = LAB.HADM_ID WHERE DIAGNOSES.LONG_TITLE = 'Candidal esophagitis' AND LAB.FLAG = 'abnormal' GROUP BY DEMOGRAPHIC.GENDER

Hardness: Extra Hard

Chart: Pie
