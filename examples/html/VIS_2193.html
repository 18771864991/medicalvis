NLQ:

  1. Create a bar chart to display the count of distinct subjects by gender for those diagnosed with 'Other and unspecified coagulation defects' and received 'ADDITIVE' drug types.

  2. How does the count of distinct subjects by gender, who are diagnosed with 'Other and unspecified coagulation defects' and received 'ADDITIVE' drug types, compare in a bar chart?

DVQ:

Visualize BAR SELECT DEMOGRAPHIC.GENDER, COUNT(DISTINCT DEMOGRAPHIC.SUBJECT_ID) FROM DEMOGRAPHIC INNER JOIN DIAGNOSES on DEMOGRAPHIC.HADM_ID = DIAGNOSES.HADM_ID INNER JOIN PRESCRIPTIONS on DEMOGRAPHIC.HADM_ID = PRESCRIPTIONS.HADM_ID WHERE DIAGNOSES.LONG_TITLE = 'Other and unspecified coagulation defects' AND PRESCRIPTIONS.DRUG_TYPE = 'ADDITIVE' GROUP BY DEMOGRAPHIC.GENDER

Hardness: Extra Hard

Chart: Bar
