NLQ:

  1. Create a line chart to display the number of unique patients prescribed 'Potassium Phosphate' for stays longer than 17 days, grouped and ordered by the length of stay.

  2. How can a line chart illustrate the count of distinct patients who were prescribed 'Potassium Phosphate' for hospital stays exceeding 17 days, with the data grouped and sorted by stay duration?

DVQ:

Visualize LINE SELECT DAYS_STAY, COUNT(DISTINCT DEMOGRAPHIC.SUBJECT_ID) FROM DEMOGRAPHIC INNER JOIN PRESCRIPTIONS ON DEMOGRAPHIC.HADM_ID = PRESCRIPTIONS.HADM_ID WHERE DAYS_STAY > 17 AND DRUG = 'Potassium Phosphate' GROUP BY DAYS_STAY ORDER BY COUNT(DISTINCT DEMOGRAPHIC.SUBJECT_ID) DESC

Hardness: Extra Hard

Chart: Line
